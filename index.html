Physics-based and fast

Motor torque-speed curves, stator current limits, wheel traction, mass and inertia are hard limits in the optimizer, so paths are fast and drivable.

Tells you what's wrong

When a path can't be made, Mayhem names the waypoint or constraint that's the problem and pins it on the field, early, while other routes are still being tried.

MayhemLib on the robot

A Java vendordep for WPILib 2026 with an API modeled on ChoreoLib. It follows paths with per-module force feedforward and needs no controller from you.

Bump recovery

If the robot is knocked off its path, MayhemLib detects the hit and takes the fastest feasible bridge back onto the trajectory.
